# Image Asset Prompts

Use these prompts in an image model to generate the required assets. Keep the same monochrome blue-gray palette.

## A. Hero Black Hole Background

**Prompt:**

Wide cinematic website hero background, dark monochrome blue-gray sci-fi aesthetic, massive black hole on the right-center with bright accretion disk sweeping horizontally across the frame, dense starfield, gravitational lensing arcs, gritty noise texture, faint scanlines, subtle code text ghosts on far left and far right, small lonely pixel-art astronaut standing near bottom center facing the black hole, dark foreground horizon, retro terminal observatory atmosphere, minimal color, high contrast, no UI text, no buttons, no people closeup, no colorful nebula, 16:9 ultra wide.

**Negative prompt:**

bright colors, purple nebula, orange fire, realistic human face, cartoon, glossy 3D, clean corporate SaaS design, large readable text, logo watermark, spaceship, planet dominating frame.

Recommended size:
- 1920×900 or 1920×1080
- crop with CSS to match screenshot

## B. Central Orb Logo

**Prompt:**

Minimal futuristic circular emblem for a dark sci-fi personal website, black disk with pale blue-white glowing event horizon ring, subtle orbit circles around it, blocky geometric letters CP or GH inside, tiny star specks inside the disk, monochrome blue-gray, high contrast, centered composition, transparent or dark background, no extra text, no watermark.

Recommended size:
- 1024×1024
- use PNG/WebP

## C. Pixel Astronaut

**Prompt:**

Small pixel-art astronaut sprite, front-facing, 16-bit retro game style, dark blue-gray and white spacesuit, black reflective visor, subtle pale blue glow under feet, isolated on transparent background, centered, no text, no scenery, crisp pixel edges.

Recommended size:
- 512×512 transparent PNG
- display at 40–50px wide

## D. Black Hole Thumbnail

**Prompt:**

Small card thumbnail image, monochrome blue-gray black hole with bright accretion disk, dense starfield, cinematic but dark, retro terminal website style, no text, no UI, no colorful nebula, 16:9.

Recommended size:
- 640×360 WebP

## E. Deep Field Thumbnail

**Prompt:**

Small card thumbnail image of a deep field star photo, dense stars and tiny distant galaxies, dark monochrome blue-gray palette, astrophotography style, subtle noise, no text, no UI, 16:9.

Recommended size:
- 640×360 WebP

## F. ASCII Astronaut / Terminal Card Graphic

**Prompt:**

Dark terminal-style line art of an astronaut helmet, made from thin pale blue-gray strokes and subtle dotted HUD elements, retro sci-fi interface panel, monochrome, transparent or very dark background, no readable words, no colorful accents.

Recommended size:
- SVG preferred, or 512×512 PNG
